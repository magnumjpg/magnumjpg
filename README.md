### Featured Public Projects

#### 1. [Spanish Urban Zoning Burden Analysis](https://github.com/magnumjpg/spanish-urban-zoning-burden-analysis)
**Domain**: Urban Econometrics, Public Choice & Housing Supply Regulation
- Panel econometric study ($N=780$, 15-year horizon, 50 representative Spanish municipalities) analyzing the causal impact of land-use discretion, bureaucratic wait times, and municipal monopoly on housing affordability.
- **Key Results**: Estimated structural housing supply elasticity ($\epsilon_s = 0.31$ under discretionary PGOU regimes vs $1.32$ in elastic benchmark), quantified deadweight loss at 14.8% of aggregate transaction value, and integrated official Bank of Spain 2026 calibration data (750,000 unit structural deficit).
- **Stack**: Python, `statsmodels`, Two-Way Fixed Effects, Monte Carlo simulation (10,000 iterations), Matplotlib/Seaborn institutional charts.

#### 2. [B2B Working Capital & CCC Control Tower](https://github.com/magnumjpg/b2b-working-capital-ccc-control-tower)
**Domain**: Corporate Finance, Treasury Analytics & Supply Chain Risk
- Automated analytical control tower for Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC), Days Sales Outstanding (DSO), and Inventory optimization across enterprise operational data.
- **Key Results**: Implemented Exhaustion (Countback) DSO for unskewed liquidity tracking, trained Random Forest credit default risk models reaching 0.92 ROC-AUC, and calculated dual-stochastic 9-cell ABC-XYZ safety buffers.
- **Stack**: DuckDB, Python, Scikit-Learn, Star-Schema dimensional modeling, statistical risk profiling.

#### 3. [B2B Revenue Operations & Margin Architecture](https://github.com/magnumjpg/b2b-revenue-operations-analytics)
**Domain**: Strategic Pricing, Commercial Operations & EBITDA Enhancement
- Quantitative framework diagnosing price realization and margin leakage across complex B2B transaction histories.
- **Key Results**: Built pocket margin waterfall models, isolated discount leakage via Stobachoff whale curves, and established CPQ governance rules driving a projected +791 bps EBITDA improvement.
- **Stack**: Python, Financial Waterfall Modeling, Pareto Analytics, Automated Reporting.
